1. The service

Postventa lets dealerships manage their appointment schedule, their vehicle fleet, their work orders with quotes, and communication with their customers. For owners it is a direct channel to their shop: see the next maintenance, book appointments, approve or reject quotes, chat, report faults and, if the shop offers it, request a tow.

Each dealership works in an isolated space: only its own staff can access its data.
