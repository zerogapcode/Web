Estoquia Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Thank you for using Estoquia. We take your privacy very seriously. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your user data when you use our mobile point of sale and inventory management application.

1. Data We Collect

To provide you with the best service and allow the application to function correctly, Estoquia collects the following data:

  • Personal Information: We collect your email address when you register and log in to our application.
  • Photos and Content: We collect the photographs of the products you choose to optionally upload to your inventory.

2. Use of Data

We use the collected information exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Account management: To authenticate your identity and ensure access to your company's data.
  • Application functionality: To allow the inventory to function properly, such as displaying your product catalog with their respective photos on the different devices in your business.

3. Data Security

All the information we collect (including emails, photos, and inventory data) is transmitted in an encrypted manner (encrypted in transit). Estoquia uses secure cloud infrastructure services to ensure that your information is protected against unauthorized access.

4. Data Deletion

You may request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. To do so, you can use the account deletion option directly in the application settings (when available) or contact us by email to arrange for the complete deletion of your data from our servers.

5. Changes to this Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this same page.

6. Contact

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us.
