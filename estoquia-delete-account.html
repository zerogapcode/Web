Account Deletion Request

At Estoquia, we respect your privacy and the control over your data. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data (including your inventory, settings, and credentials), you can request it as follows:

1. Through the App:
If you have the app installed, go to your profile settings within the app and select the "Delete Account" option. The data will be permanently erased from our servers.

2. Via Email:
If you no longer have access to the application, you can request the manual deletion of your data by sending us an email from the address linked to your account.
